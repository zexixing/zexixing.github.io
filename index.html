Current Focus

Small bodies in ultraviolet light

My work sits between planetary science, observatory operations, calibration, and software. The scientific question is simple to state but hard to measure: how do small bodies release water and other volatiles as they evolve?

Research

Volatiles, interstellar comets, and UV toolchains

Cometary Water And Volatile Evolution

Water is my central tracer for understanding cometary activity. I use near-ultraviolet OH emission, mainly with Swift/UVOT, to measure water production and follow how cometary ices are exposed, depleted, and redistributed through the coma.

Interstellar Comets

I led the Swift water measurements for 2I/Borisov and 3I/ATLAS. These campaigns connect individual detections to time evolution: from 2I's unusually fast post-perihelion decline to 3I's transition through CO2-rich, H2O-rich, and CO-rich activity regimes.

UV Small-Body Observing Toolchain

Much of my work is at the interface between science and operations: planning observations for non-sidereal targets, removing field-star contamination, reducing motion smearing with event mode, adapting grism extraction for extended sources, and turning these methods into reusable software such as uvotimgpy.
